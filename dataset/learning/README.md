# Synthetic learning-cycle fixtures

These two fictional DOCX files are separate from the six original incoming scenarios and the held-out answer key. Rebuild them with `python3 -m scripts.build_learning_fixtures`.

- **L01 — Maple Metrics:** A seven-year encrypted archive may be retained only for a named regulatory duty, without business access. Use this as the first review. A synthetic reviewer can record a conditional decision, then explicitly publish the selected passage with context and reuse limits.
- **L02 — Harbour Analytics:** Similar archive wording permits commercial-team product analytics. The L01 decision must appear as contextual precedent after publication, while L02 keeps its own review route and needs its own human judgement.

After L01's publication is withdrawn, it must disappear from active retrieval and remain visible in L01's history. These fixtures test the workflow boundary; they are not legal opinions or evidence of real-world retrieval quality.
